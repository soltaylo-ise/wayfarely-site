Wayfarely
In development

Trips planned to the budget you actually set.

Tell Wayfarely where you're going, when, and what you can spend. It returns a day-by-day itinerary with real costs — flights, lodging, meals, activities — that adds up to the number you gave it.

±5%

Every itinerary is built to land within five percent of your stated budget, with the arithmetic shown by category rather than hidden behind an estimate.

How it works

Three inputs, one itinerary. No account juggling, no twelve tabs of price comparison.

01

Describe the trip

Departure city, one destination or several, your dates, group size, and a total budget. Interests and pace, if you have opinions about them.

02

Wayfarely plans it

Days are laid out with timed activities, places to eat, transport between them, and where to stay — each with a cost attached.

03

Take it with you

Read it on your phone, export it as a PDF, or keep several versions of a trip side by side before you commit.

Two ways to plan

Longer, multi-city trips take more research — so they're priced by how much planning they actually need, not a flat fee.
